THE FUNDAMENTALS

Safety is in the details.

Use these core practices as a quick reference for daily routines and inspection preparation.

🍎🥦🥛🥩🍞Keep every ingredient fresh, clean and safe.
🥗
🧼
01 / HYGIENE

Cleanliness

Keep kitchen surfaces, equipment and utensils clean. Wash hands regularly, especially before handling food.

🧊
02 / TEMPERATURE

Food storage

Store food at suitable temperatures and separate raw ingredients from ready-to-eat food.

🍳
03 / PREPARATION

Safe preparation

Cook food thoroughly and use clean equipment to prevent cross-contamination.

🧑‍🍳
04 / PEOPLE

Personal hygiene

Wear clean clothing, maintain hand hygiene and cover cuts properly while working.

📋
05 / COMPLIANCE

Record keeping

Document inspection findings clearly and follow up on issues until they are resolved.

🐜
06 / PREVENTION

Pest prevention

Keep food covered, remove waste regularly and check storage areas for signs of pests.
